Metal Detecting App Features — Everything You Need in the Field

GPS Metal Detecting Hunt Tracker

Track your metal detecting hunts with GPS — see where you've walked, which grids you've covered, and how efficient your sweeps are. Works completely offline.

Log Metal Detecting Finds with Photos & GPS

Log every metal detecting find with photos, depth, materials, and precise GPS coordinates. Your entire discovery history stays organized and searchable forever.

Metal Detecting Map Overlays

Layer historic plats, aerials, LiDAR terrain, and custom images over modern maps to find forgotten homesites, old paths, and buried structures.

Metal Detecting Permissions Made Easy

PermissionLink™ — digital land permissions with QR approval, expiration windows, and automatic find sharing. Getting metal detecting permission shouldn't be awkward.

Responsible Finders Network

Submit discoveries to museums and historical societies. UK detectorists get built-in PAS and Treasure Act reporting.

Apple Watch Companion

Control your hunt from your wrist. Log finds, check stats, and use hands-free gestures — no phone required.
