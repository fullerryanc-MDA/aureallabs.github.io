Solve the Problems That Hold Most Detectorists Back

Stop Missing Ground — Track Exactly Where You've Searched

Most detectorists think they've covered a site thoroughly, but without a visual reference, it's easy to miss large sections or overlap the same ground repeatedly. Aureal records your GPS trail in real time, builds a live coverage heatmap, and shows your past hunt paths when you return — so you know exactly what's been covered and what's left.

Learn how to stop re-detecting the same ground →

Keep a Complete Record of Every Find (So Nothing Gets Lost or Forgotten)

A find without context is just a piece of metal. Aureal lets you log every discovery with photos, depth, material, GPS coordinates, detector settings, and VDI numbers. Over time, your find history becomes a searchable record that helps you understand patterns, compare equipment, and remember exactly where and how you found each item.

See how tracking your setup reveals what's actually working →

Find Old Sites That Aren't Visible Anymore

The best detecting sites are often invisible from the ground — old foundations, vanished paths, forgotten gathering spots. Aureal lets you overlay LiDAR terrain, historical aerials, soil maps, and custom images directly onto your live hunt map, aligning the past with the present so you can detect exactly where something actually happened.

Learn how to use overlays to find what's hidden →

Get Permission to Metal Detect Without Awkward Conversations

Access is the biggest barrier in metal detecting — not because land is unavailable, but because asking feels uncomfortable. PermissionLink™ lets you create a clear request with mapped boundaries and share it as a QR code or link. Landowners approve with a tap, set conditions, and get automatic reports after your hunt. No more handshake deals that get forgotten.

Read the full guide to getting permission →

Report Finds Responsibly — RFN & PAS Built In

Found something significant? Submit discoveries to museums, historical societies, and research institutions through the Responsible Finders Network. UK detectorists get built-in PAS reporting with automatic FLO contact lookup and Treasure Act deadline tracking — so you never miss a legal reporting window.

Stay Hands-Free — Apple Watch & Lock Screen Widgets

Every time you pull out your phone to log something, you break your flow. Aureal's Apple Watch integration gives you large, field-ready buttons to log finds and flag signals from your wrist. A lock screen widget shows your live hunt stats at a glance. Your tools should support your detecting, not interrupt it.
