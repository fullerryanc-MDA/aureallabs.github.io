Our Mission

Aureal is built for detectorists who care about the hobby. We provide the tools you need to track your progress, document your discoveries, and explore with purpose.

Whether you're mapping a new site with historic overlays, logging a significant find, or tracking your lifetime stats — Aureal keeps everything organized and private.

Live Hunt Tracking

Find Logging

Catalog every discovery with photos, GPS coordinates, depth, and material details.

Historic Map Overlays

Badges & Milestones

Lifetime Statistics

Eco Impact Tracking
