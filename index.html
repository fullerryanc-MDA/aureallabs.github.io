Everything You Need in the Field

Live Hunt Tracking

See where you've walked, which grids you've covered, and how efficient your sweeps are — even completely offline.

Find Logging That Feels Effortless

Snap photos, record depth, tag materials, and save precise GPS coordinates. Your entire discovery stays organized forever.

Historic & Custom Map Overlays

Layer old plats, aerials, and imported historic images over modern maps to uncover forgotten paths, homesites, and structures.

PermissionLink™

Digital land permissions with QR approval, expiration windows, and optional owner anonymity. Getting permission shouldn't be awkward.

Badges & Milestones

Celebrate progress with meaningful achievements: hunts completed, eco-impact, rare finds, lifetime streaks, and more.

Eco Impact Tracking

See the difference you make — trash removed, sites cleaned, percentage of responsible recovery — and help elevate the hobby's reputation.
