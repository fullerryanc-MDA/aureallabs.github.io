Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Aureal Labs LLC ("Aureal," "we," "our," or "us"), your privacy is fundamental to everything we build. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Aureal mobile application and related services.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you use Aureal, you may provide:

  • Account information (email address, username)
  • Hunt session data (GPS tracks, timestamps, duration)
  • Find logs (photos, descriptions, depth, GPS coordinates, material tags)
  • Custom overlays and map data you import
  • Permission records (PermissionLink data)

Information Collected Automatically

We automatically collect:

  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • App usage data (features used, session length)
  • Crash reports and performance data

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve Aureal's features
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Generate your personal statistics and achievements
  • Provide customer support
  • Send important service updates

What We Never Do

We never:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your find locations with other users without explicit consent
  • Use your data for advertising purposes
  • Share landowner information from PermissionLink with anyone

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Hunt data and finds are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to our secure cloud infrastructure. You can export or delete your data at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Export your data in standard formats
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

Children's Privacy

Aureal is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes via the app or email.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

privacy@aureallabs.com
