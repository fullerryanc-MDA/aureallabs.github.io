Delete Your Account

Request permanent deletion of your Aureal account and all associated data.

This action is permanent and irreversible

Once your account is deleted, all data is permanently removed from our systems and cannot be recovered.

What will be deleted

  • Account information Profile, email address, and login credentials
  • All finds and photos Every find you've logged, including photos, GPS coordinates, and metadata
  • Hunt history GPS paths, coverage data, stats, and all hunt records
  • Achievements and badges All progress, badges, and reputation scores
  • Subscriptions and permissions Active subscriptions, PermissionLink records, and site listings
  • Overlays and uploaded images Historic map overlays, uploaded images, and cached map data

How to request deletion

Option 1: In the app

The fastest way to delete your account:

  1. Open Aureal and go to Profile
  2. Tap Delete Account
  3. Type DELETE and enter your password to confirm
  4. Your account and all data will be deleted immediately

Option 2: Email request

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email to:

support@aureallabs.com

Include the email address associated with your account. We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 3 business days.

Data retention policy

After deletion, your data is immediately removed from production systems. Backup copies are automatically purged within 30 days in accordance with GDPR requirements. Anonymized, aggregated analytics data (which cannot identify you) may be retained.
