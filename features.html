Everything You Need in the Field

Aureal combines powerful hunt tracking, intuitive find logging, and responsible reporting tools in one beautifully designed app.

Core Feature

Live Hunt Tracking

See exactly where you've been, where you haven't, and how efficiently you're covering ground — all in real-time.

  • GPS breadcrumb trails that show your exact path
  • Coverage heatmaps to identify missed areas
  • Works completely offline — no cell signal needed
  • Session duration, distance, and pace tracking
  • Resume hunts across multiple sessions
Core Feature

Find Logging That Feels Effortless

Document every discovery with photos, depth, materials, GPS coordinates, and notes — all organized and searchable forever.

  • Quick-capture with smart defaults
  • Multiple photos per find
  • Category tags: coin, relic, jewelry, trash, and more
  • Depth recording in inches or centimeters
  • Search and filter your entire find history
Pro Feature

Historic & Custom Map Overlays

Layer old plats, aerials, and research images over modern maps to uncover forgotten structures, paths, and homesites.

  • Import any image as a custom overlay
  • Two-point anchoring for precision alignment
  • Adjustable opacity for easy comparison
  • Toggle overlays during active hunts
  • LiDAR data support for terrain analysis

Research like a pro — hunt like you know what's there.

Core Feature

PermissionLink™

Getting permission shouldn't be awkward. PermissionLink lets landowners approve detecting with a simple scan — no phone calls, no paperwork.

  • QR code for instant in-person permission capture
  • Digital permission records with timestamps
  • Landowners set terms, time windows, and boundaries
  • Verified permission status visible during hunts
  • Share find reports with landowners automatically

Responsible detecting should be easy. Now it is.

Scout+ Feature

Responsible Finders Network

Submit discoveries to museums, historical societies, and UK Finds Liaison Officers — all from the app. Build your reputation as a responsible detectorist.

  • Submit finds to US museums and historical societies
  • UK PAS and Treasure Act reporting built-in
  • Privacy-first: GPS anonymized to county level
  • Track submission status and institution responses
  • Verified RFN member status for your profile

Do right by history — and get recognized for it.

Pro / Elite Feature

Apple Watch Companion

Control your hunt from your wrist. Start, pause, and log finds without pulling out your phone — even with dirty hands.

  • Live hunt status at a glance
  • Quick find logging from your wrist
  • Session stats: time, distance, find count
  • Hands-free gesture controls (Elite)

Your phone stays in your pocket. Your focus stays on the hunt.

Elite Feature

AR Target Visualization

Point your camera at the ground and see exactly where your target is buried. Precision recovery meets augmented reality.

  • Visual target marker overlaid on camera view
  • Depth indicator based on your detector's reading
  • Smaller plugs, faster recovery
  • Works with any metal detector

Stop guessing. Start pinpointing.
