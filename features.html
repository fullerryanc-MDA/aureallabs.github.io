Everything You Need in the Field

Aureal combines powerful hunt tracking, intuitive find logging, and historic research tools in one beautifully designed app.

Core Feature

Live Hunt Tracking

See exactly where you've been, where you haven't, and how efficiently you're covering ground — all in real-time.

  • GPS breadcrumb trails that show your exact path
  • Coverage analysis to identify missed areas
  • Works completely offline — no cell signal needed
  • Session duration, distance, and pace tracking
  • Resume hunts across multiple sessions
Core Feature

Find Logging That Feels Effortless

Document every discovery with photos, depth, materials, GPS coordinates, and notes — all organized and searchable forever.

  • Quick-capture with smart defaults
  • Multiple photos per find
  • Material tags: coin, relic, jewelry, trash, and more
  • Depth recording in inches or centimeters
  • Search and filter your entire find history
Pro Feature

Historic & Custom Map Overlays

Layer old plats, aerials, and research images over modern maps to uncover forgotten structures, paths, and homesites.

  • Import any image as a custom overlay
  • Two-point anchoring for precision alignment
  • Adjustable opacity for easy comparison
  • Toggle overlays during active hunts
  • LiDAR data support for terrain analysis

No other detecting app gives users this level of research power.

Coming Soon

PermissionLink™

Getting permission shouldn't be awkward. PermissionLink lets landowners approve detecting with a simple tap — no phone calls, no paperwork.

  • Send secure digital permission requests
  • QR code sharing for in-person requests
  • Landowners set time windows and no-go zones
  • Real-time permission status in-app
  • All data encrypted — landowner info never sold

Responsible detecting should be easy. Now it is.

Pro Feature

Apple Watch Companion

Control your hunt from your wrist. Start, pause, and log finds without pulling out your phone.

  • Gesture controls for hands-free operation
  • Live hunt status at a glance
  • Quick find logging from your wrist
  • Session stats: time, distance, finds

And Much More

Badges & Milestones

Lifetime Stats & Trends

Track hours hunted, distance covered, find categories, grid efficiency, and how your skills evolve over time.

Eco Impact Tracking

See the difference you make — trash removed, sites cleaned, percentage of responsible recovery.

Privacy-First
