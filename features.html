Metal Detecting App Features — GPS Tracking, Find Logging & Map Overlays

Aureal is the all-in-one metal detecting tracker — GPS hunt tracking, find logging with photos, historic map overlays, grid search tools, and digital land permissions.

Core Feature

GPS Metal Detecting Hunt Tracker

Track your metal detecting hunts with real-time GPS — see exactly where you've been, where you haven't, and how efficiently you're covering ground.

  • GPS breadcrumb trails that show your exact path
  • Coverage heatmaps to identify missed areas
  • Works completely offline — no cell signal needed
  • Session duration, distance, and pace tracking
  • Resume hunts across multiple sessions
Core Feature

Log Metal Detecting Finds with Photos & GPS

Log every metal detecting find with photos, depth, materials, GPS coordinates, and notes — all organized and searchable forever.

  • Quick-capture with smart defaults
  • Multiple photos per find
  • Category tags: coin, relic, jewelry, trash, and more
  • Depth recording in inches or centimeters
  • Search and filter your entire find history
Core Feature

History & Finds — Your Permanent Detecting Logbook

Every hunt, every find, every detail — searchable and stored forever. Aureal builds a personal archive of your detecting career that you can revisit, filter, and learn from.

  • Full-text search across all finds by name, category, or location
  • Filter by category, metal type, date range, or hunt
  • Detailed hunt timelines with GPS trail, duration, and distance
  • Every find linked to its hunt, location, and photos
  • Cloud-synced — access your logbook from the app or PRO Portal

Never lose track of a find again.

Pro Feature

Metal Detecting Map Overlays — Historic, LiDAR & Custom

Layer historic plats, aerial photos, LiDAR terrain, and custom images over modern maps to find forgotten structures, old paths, and buried homesites.

  • Import any image as a custom overlay
  • Two-point anchoring for precision alignment
  • Adjustable opacity for easy comparison
  • Toggle overlays during active hunts
  • LiDAR data support for terrain analysis

Research like a pro — hunt like you know what's there.

Core Feature

Metal Detecting Land Permissions — PermissionLink™

Getting metal detecting permission shouldn't be awkward. PermissionLink lets landowners approve detecting with a simple QR scan — no phone calls, no paperwork.

  • QR code for instant in-person permission capture
  • Digital permission records with timestamps
  • Landowners set terms, time windows, and boundaries
  • Verified permission status visible during hunts
  • Share find reports with landowners automatically

Responsible detecting should be easy. Now it is.

Pro Feature

Responsible Finders Network

Submit discoveries to museums, historical societies, and UK Finds Liaison Officers — all from the app. Build your reputation as a responsible detectorist.

  • Submit finds to US museums and historical societies
  • UK PAS and Treasure Act reporting built-in
  • Privacy-first: GPS anonymized to county level
  • Track submission status and institution responses
  • Verified RFN member status for your profile

Do right by history — and get recognized for it.

Pro Feature

Apple Watch Companion

Control your hunt from your wrist. Start, pause, and log finds without pulling out your phone — even with dirty hands.

  • Live hunt status at a glance
  • Quick find logging from your wrist
  • Session stats: time, distance, find count
  • Complications for watch face at-a-glance stats

Your phone stays in your pocket. Your focus stays on the hunt.

PRO Portal — Your Hunt Command Center

Research on a big screen. Hunt with confidence in the field. The PRO Portal is a full web dashboard included with every Pro subscription.

One-Click Overlays

Generate Overlays from Government Data

No more searching for old maps online. The PRO Portal pulls directly from USGS, NAIP, and SSURGO databases to generate publication-quality overlays in seconds — then syncs them to your phone.

  • LiDAR terrain: Hillshade, multi-directional hillshade, slope, aspect, contour lines, and elevation tinting from USGS 3DEP
  • Historic aerials: NHAP photos from the 1980s and current NAIP imagery at 0.6m resolution
  • Soil surveys: SSURGO soil composition and texture data to identify high-potential ground
  • Land boundaries: PLSS Township and Section grids for property research
  • Per-layer opacity controls, real-time preview, and instant sync to your phone
AI-Powered Analytics

Hunt History, Finds, & AI Summaries

Review every hunt in detail — trail maps, find galleries, coverage stats, and AI-generated performance insights that help you improve.

  • Interactive trail maps with color-coded paths, find markers, and start/end points
  • AI hunt summaries with key highlights and suggestions for improvement
  • Advanced find filtering by category, metal type, depth, and notable status
  • Full photo gallery, editable find details, and tile or list view modes
Location Intelligence

Spot Ranking & Trail Stacking

Aureal automatically groups your hunts into locations and ranks them — so you always know which spots are worth returning to.

  • Spots ranked by finds per hour, coverage percentage, time invested, and total XP
  • Stacked trail mode overlays every hunt at a location to visualize total coverage
  • Individual trail mode to review specific hunts at a site
  • Interactive map with click-to-select spots and detailed stats panels
Equipment & Achievements

Detector Profiles, Badges & Data Export

Track your equipment performance, earn 50+ achievement badges, and export all your data whenever you want.

  • Manage detectors with brand, model, coil size, and coil type — synced to your phone
  • 50+ badges across Discovery, Eco Impact, Consistency, Exploration, and Milestones
  • Full data export in JSON or CSV — your data is always yours
  • Monthly progress charts, eco impact dashboard, and lifetime stats
