Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial?

The Free tier lets you try Aureal's core features forever. When you're ready for cloud backup and more, upgrading is just a tap away.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards through the App Store. Your subscription is managed through your Apple account settings.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel anytime through your App Store settings. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

Do you offer refunds?

Refund requests are handled by the App Store according to Apple's policies. We're happy to help you navigate the process.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Your data is always safe. If you downgrade, cloud-synced data remains accessible in read-only mode. Local data stays on your device forever.

We're here to help. Reach out anytime.
