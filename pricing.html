Frequently Asked Questions

Can I try before I subscribe?

The Free tier lets you use Aureal's core features forever — unlimited hunts, find logging, GPS tracking, and heatmaps. Upgrade to Pro when you're ready for cloud sync and more.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel anytime through your App Store settings. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store. Your subscription is handled through your Apple account settings.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Your data is always safe. If you downgrade, cloud-synced data remains accessible in read-only mode. Local data stays on your device forever.

Is there an annual discount?

Yes! The annual plan saves you 17% compared to monthly billing — $49.99/year instead of $59.88 if you paid monthly.

Do you offer refunds?

Refund requests are handled by the App Store according to Apple's policies. We're happy to help you navigate the process.
