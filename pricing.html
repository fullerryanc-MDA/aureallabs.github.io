Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial?

The Free tier lets you try Aureal's core features forever. When you're ready for more, upgrading is just a tap away.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards through the App Store and Google Play. Your subscription is managed through your device's app store.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel anytime through your app store settings. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

Do you offer refunds?

Refund requests are handled by the App Store or Google Play according to their policies. We're happy to help you navigate the process.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Your data is always safe. If you downgrade, you'll still have access to view everything — you just won't be able to add new items beyond your plan limits.

We're here to help. Reach out anytime.
