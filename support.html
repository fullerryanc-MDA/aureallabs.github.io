Getting Started

Features

Import any image (old plat maps, historic aerials, survey documents) and align it with modern maps using our pan-zoom-confirm anchoring system. Toggle between map styles and overlays to compare past and present during active hunts to guide your search.

PermissionLink is our digital permission system that makes getting landowner approval simple. Send a request, share a QR code, and landowners can approve with conditions (time windows, no-go zones) all digitally. No awkward conversations, no paperwork, and all data stays private and encrypted.

When you make a find, tap to log it. Add photos, record depth, tag the material type (iron, brass, steel, etc.) and category (coin, relic, jewelry, trash, etc.), and the GPS coordinates are saved automatically. Add notes, and your entire find history becomes searchable and organized forever.

The Apple Watch companion lets you control hunts without pulling out your phone. Start, pause, and end sessions, see live stats (time, distance, find count), and quickly log finds using gesture controls. It's perfect for keeping your phone protected while you dig.

Account & Billing

Upgrading is easy! Go to Settings > Subscription in the app and choose your new plan. Your upgrade takes effect immediately, and you'll be credited for any unused time on your current plan.

You can cancel anytime through your device's app store (App Store for iOS, Google Play for Android). Go to your subscriptions settings and cancel Aureal. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

Your data is never deleted. If you downgrade, you can still view all your existing hunts, finds, and overlays. You just won't be able to add new items beyond your plan's limits until you upgrade again.

Yes! Your Aureal account syncs across all your devices. Log in on your phone, tablet, web browser, or even switch between iOS and Android — your hunts, finds, and settings follow you everywhere.
